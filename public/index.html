B!bluffmates
LIVE PARTY GAME
Made for curious minds & confident liars.2–8 players · no accounts · just a room code
THE QUICK RULES

Tell a good lie.
Spot an even better one.

  1. Join your friends with a room code.
  2. Everyone writes a believable fake answer to a curious question.
  3. Read the mixed answers and vote for the real one.
  4. Find the truth for 100 points. Every vote for your bluff earns you 50 points.
  5. After the chosen number of rounds, the highest score wins.

You can’t vote for your own answer. Keep bluffs kind and clever.
